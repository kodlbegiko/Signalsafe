# Security and privacy boundaries

- No authentication or account creation.
- No remote analytics, advertising or telemetry.
- No cloud model API.
- No collection of name, school, class, email or phone.
- Emergency mode never asks for real suspicious messages, screenshots, credentials, OTP or financial data.
- Local exports are initiated by the user.
- Imported JSON is schema-checked before replacing local state.
- The product never declares a message completely safe.

For formal testing, participant contact details and consent records must remain outside the public repository and separate from anonymous response records.
